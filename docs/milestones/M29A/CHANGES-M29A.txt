Milestone 29A
=============

* Added Win32-host VIOCALLS.dll and KBDCALLS.dll personality modules.
* Added historical-ordinal exports needed by the current shell:
    KbdCharIn @4, KbdFlushBuffer @13
    VioScrollUp @7, VioGetCurPos @9, VioSetCurPos @15,
    VioWrtTTY @19, VioGetMode @21

* Moved PAUSE through KbdFlushBuffer/KbdCharIn and VioWrtTTY.
* Moved CLS through VioGetMode/VioScrollUp/VioSetCurPos.
* Added console-boundary-check to keep Win32 console APIs out of CMD proper.
* Added m29a-console-test.cmd native regression.
* Added vio-kbd-probe.c archaeology test for the historical C/386 VIO/KBD
  import/fixup form.
* Kept the genuine C/386 backend on temporary DosRead/DosWrite console
  fallbacks until that thunk form is captured; no guessed 16:32 ABI was added.
* Retains M28E direct loading of the supported flat-32 LE and LX subsets.
