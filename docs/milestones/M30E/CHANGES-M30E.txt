Milestone 30E
-------------

* Identified DOSCALLS ordinal 354 as DosSetExceptionHandler.
* Added the two-dword OS/2 EXCEPTIONREGISTRATIONRECORD view.
* Added a single-thread guest exception registration chain for the current
  M30 EMX execution model.
* DosSetExceptionHandler links the supplied record at the chain head, fills
  prev_structure, mirrors the head in tib_pexchain, and returns NO_ERROR.
* Added ordinal 355 DosUnsetExceptionHandler as the matching teardown path.
* Added opt-in `M30E EXCEPT:` tracing under OS2_TRACE_EMX_SELF.
* Does not yet translate or dispatch Win32 exceptions to guest OS/2 handlers.
* Keeps M30C a.out relocation and M30D TIB stack behavior unchanged.
