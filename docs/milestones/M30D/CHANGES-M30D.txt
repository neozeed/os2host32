Milestone 30D
-------------

* Identified the M30C `Stack size too small` diagnostic as a false positive
  caused by the compatibility DosGetInfoBlocks implementation returning a
  zero-filled TIB.
* Confirmed EMX reads TIB+4 / TIB+8 (tib_pstack / tib_pstacklimit) and requires
  a span greater than 0x4000 during startup.
* Added private OS2HOST32 export OS2HostQueryMainStack.
* Publishes the actual mapped main LX stack bounds immediately after mapping
  the executable and before guest DLL initialization.
* DosGetInfoBlocks now populates tib_pstack/tib_pstacklimit with those guest
  bounds instead of exposing a zero-byte stack.
* Added a minimal TIB2 with TID 1 and hooked tib_ptib2 to it.
* Added opt-in M30D TIB tracing under OS2_TRACE_EMX_SELF.
* Keeps the M30C a.out-sidecar relocation mechanism unchanged.
* No mutation of the bound EMX executable is required for the stack test.
