Milestone 23
============

* Added C89 `cmdparse.c` / `cmdparse.h` semantic reconstruction of the
  Dec-1991 NT CMD `clex.c` and `cparse.c` architecture.
* Preserved recovered parser levels ParseS0..ParseS5 and exact operator
  precedence: &, ||, &&, |, redirection, command/group.
* Added quote-aware metacharacter lexing and `%NAME%` environment expansion at
  the recovered SubVar/MSEnvVar boundary.
* Added parentheses, @ prefix, REM handling, redirection AST nodes, parse errors.
* CMD32OS2 now executes &, &&, ||, groups through the parser AST.
* Pipelines and redirection are parsed but not executed yet.
* Added `cmd32os2 --parse ...` AST diagnostics independent of DOSCALLS.dll.
* Added portable `parse-test.c` and `make parser-check` regression.
* Added original clex/cparse disassembly ranges and recovery notes under
  analysis/.
* Updated MinGW and MSC8/VC1-oriented build paths to link cmdparse.c.
