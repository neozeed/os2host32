Milestone 30F
-------------

* Identified DOSCALLS ordinal 378 as DosSetSignalExceptionFocus.
* Added single-process signal-exception focus nesting semantics.
* enable=1 acquires focus, enable=0 releases it, and pulTimes receives the
  resulting nesting count.
* Added DOSCALLS ordinal 418 DosAcknowledgeSignalException as the paired
  acknowledgement path likely to follow focus acquisition during runtime init.
* Added opt-in `M30F SIGNAL:` tracing under OS2_TRACE_EMX_SELF.
* Host Ctrl-C/Ctrl-Break delivery into the guest exception chain is not yet
  implemented; M30F supplies normal startup bookkeeping only.
* Keeps M30C a.out relocation, M30D TIB stack, and M30E exception-chain
  registration behavior unchanged.
